A Pages CMS alternative for Astro sites

Markstage vs Pages CMS: both edit files in git. Markstage edits on your Astro site's real design and adds roles, staging and forms. Checked 3 Nov 2026.

Facts about Pages CMS checked on 2026-11-03, from its own website. Markstage is not affiliated with it.

Pages CMS calls itself "the simplest CMS you'll ever need": it edits the content and media in your GitHub repository, with no database, no API and no extra backend for the content. Markstage does more, and needs more: writers edit on the page, and its server adds roles, staging and a forms inbox.

Who each one is for

Pages CMS suits small sites on GitHub that want a free, simple editor, used online at app.pagescms.org or self-hosted.

Markstage suits teams whose writers should edit on the real design and who need roles, review, single sign-on and site tools in one place. Markstage is pre-release.

Where Pages CMS is stronger

  • Simplicity. Sign in with GitHub and start editing; there is a free hosted version.
  • Any generator. It works with Jekyll, Hugo, Next.js, Astro and others.
  • Mobile. Its site lists a full responsive design.

Moving from Pages CMS to Markstage

Your files stay in place. Your Pages CMS configuration names a folder for each kind of content; add the same folders as collections in markstage.config.mjs and add the @markstage/astro integration. Invite your editors to Markstage by email; they no longer need a GitHub account.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pages CMS free?

Yes. Its site says it is free whether you use the online version, deploy it on Vercel or self-host it.

Does Pages CMS work with Astro?

Yes. Astro's docs have a Pages CMS guide.

Does Pages CMS support GitLab?

Its FAQ says GitHub only, for now.

What does Markstage add over Pages CMS?

Editing on the site's real design, roles and custom roles, single sign-on, staging and production, releases, scheduled publishing, a forms inbox, SEO checks and translation status.
