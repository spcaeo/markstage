CloudCannon is the product closest to Markstage. Both keep content as files in git, and both let writers edit text where it appears on the site. The big difference: CloudCannon is a hosted service with monthly plans, and Markstage runs on your own server for free.

Who each one is for

CloudCannon suits teams and agencies who want everything hosted: the editor, the builds, hosting on a CDN, forms and support. It is a featured CMS partner in Astro's docs.

Markstage suits teams who want the same way of working (git files, editing on the page) but on their own server, with no per-user fees, and with staging, custom roles and single sign-on included. Markstage is pre-release.

Where CloudCannon is stronger

  • Nothing to run. CloudCannon builds and hosts the site and runs the editor. Markstage needs your own server.
  • Hosting and CDN included, with automatic HTTPS and DDoS protection on its plans.
  • Support and services. Priority and dedicated support, onboarding, and a migration service.
  • Many generators. CloudCannon works with more than Astro; Markstage has one adapter today, Astro; adapters beyond Astro are planned (roadmap).
  • Maturity. CloudCannon has years of production use; Markstage is pre-release.

Moving from CloudCannon to Markstage

Your content is already in your git repository, so nothing has to be exported. List the same folders as collections in markstage.config.mjs and add the @markstage/astro integration. CloudCannon's editable regions are HTML data attributes and web components; Markstage uses its own field bindings, emitted only in editor mode, so you mark the same fields again. Forms: point the site's forms at Markstage (/markstage/forms/<id>). Then host the built site wherever you like and run the Markstage server next to it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a self-hosted alternative to CloudCannon?

Yes. Markstage is git-based like CloudCannon, edits on the site's design, and runs on your own server. It is free to self-host. CloudCannon does not list a self-hosted version.

How much does CloudCannon cost?

On 3 November 2026 its pricing page listed Standard at $55 a month ($49 a month paid yearly) with 3 users, Team at $350 a month ($300 yearly) with 15 users, and Enterprise at a custom price. Extra users are $10 a month each.

Does CloudCannon work with Astro?

Yes. Astro's docs have a CloudCannon guide, and CloudCannon has an @cloudcannon/editable-regions package for live editing of Astro components.

What does Markstage not do that CloudCannon does?

It does not host your site or run your builds as a service, and it has no paid support plans or migration service today.
