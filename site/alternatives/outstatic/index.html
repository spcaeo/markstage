An Outstatic alternative for Astro sites

Markstage vs Outstatic: both git-based and free to self-host. Markstage edits on your Astro site's real design and includes roles. Checked 3 Nov 2026.

Facts about Outstatic checked on 2026-11-03, from its own website. Markstage is not affiliated with it.

Outstatic gives Markdown and MDX files a real editing dashboard, with content kept in your GitHub repository. Its Pro plan adds hosted publishing, email and Google sign-in, roles and AI. Markstage is self-hosted only, edits on the page, and includes roles and single sign-on at no cost.

Who each one is for

Outstatic suits developers who want a quick dashboard for Markdown and MDX on GitHub, self-hosted for free or hosted on Pro.

Markstage suits teams whose writers should edit on an Astro site's real design, with roles, staging and site tools included. Markstage is pre-release.

Where Outstatic is stronger

  • MDX. Outstatic edits MDX; Markstage does not.
  • A hosted plan. Outstatic Pro runs the dashboard for you.
  • Setup speed. Its site promises a CMS on your repo in about five minutes.

Moving from Outstatic to Markstage

Your Markdown files are in your repository already. Add their folders as collections in markstage.config.mjs and add the @markstage/astro integration. Keep MDX files where they are, or convert them to Markdown. Invite your editors to Markstage; each save is then committed in their own name.

Frequently asked questions

Is Outstatic free?

Self-hosted, yes. Hosted projects on outstatic.com need Pro to save and publish.

Does Outstatic work with Astro?

Its site lists Astro among the generators it works with. It is not in Astro's own list of CMS guides.

Who appears as the commit author?

With Outstatic, edits made through email or Google sign-in are committed as the Outstatic Bot. With Markstage, the signed-in person is the git author of each save.
