A Statamic alternative for Astro sites

Markstage vs Statamic: both keep content as files in git. Markstage edits on your Astro site's design with no PHP and no licence fee. Checked 3 Nov 2026.

Facts about Statamic checked on 2026-11-03, from its own website. Markstage is not affiliated with it.

Statamic is a flat-file CMS on Laravel: content and configuration live in files and can be version-controlled with git. With Astro, you read it through its REST or GraphQL API, which is part of Statamic Pro. Markstage edits an Astro site's own Markdown and JSON files, on the site's design.

Who each one is for

Statamic suits PHP and Laravel teams who want a polished flat-file CMS, often drawing the site with Statamic itself, or using it headless.

Markstage suits Astro sites that want writers on the real design and no PHP application to run. Markstage is pre-release.

Where Statamic is stronger

  • Laravel: extend it with the whole Laravel ecosystem.
  • Polish and maturity, with a marketplace and developer support on Pro.
  • Multi-site and multilingual editing on Pro.

Moving from Statamic to Markstage

Statamic already keeps content in flat files. Where they are Markdown with front matter, Markstage can edit them where they are, once their folder is a collection in markstage.config.mjs. Other formats need converting to Markdown or JSON first.

Frequently asked questions

Does Statamic work with Astro?

Yes, headless: Astro's Statamic guide uses its REST or GraphQL API. Statamic's pricing page lists the headless APIs under Pro.

Is Statamic free?

Statamic Core is free with one admin user. Pro is $349 per site, including a year of updates, then $99 a year.

Is there a flat-file CMS for Astro without PHP?

Markstage runs on Node next to your Astro site and keeps content as Markdown and JSON in git.
