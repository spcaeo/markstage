Which is the best CMS for Astro? Markstage compared

The best CMS for Astro depends on your team: who edits, where content lives and what you want to run. Here is where Markstage, a visual editor that keeps content in git, fits next to hosted and git-based CMSs, and where each one leads. Markstage is not affiliated with any of them.

Astro gives content collections (Markdown, MDX, JSON and YAML with a schema) but no editor of its own; its docs send you to a CMS. Markstage is that editor for teams who want writers to work on the site's real design, every save to change only what was edited (with the Markstage server, a commit by the person who saved), and staging, roles, single sign-on and a forms inbox included, all on their own server for free. CloudCannon is the closest hosted product: also git-based, with editing on a preview of the built site, from $55 a month. TinaCMS gives click-to-edit on Astro pages and edits MDX, which Markstage does not. Decap CMS needs no server at all and Keystatic runs inside your Astro site, but with both, writers fill in forms rather than editing the page itself. Markstage is pre-release; most products here have years of use and bigger communities.

Where others lead (honestly)

  • MDX. Markstage edits Markdown with front matter, and JSON. A site built on MDX components fits TinaCMS, Keystatic or Outstatic better today.
  • No server at all. Decap CMS and Sveltia CMS run in the browser against your git host. Markstage needs Node.js for a team (sign-in, publishing, the inbox); alone on your computer it runs inside astro dev.
  • A hosted service. CloudCannon, TinaCloud, Storyblok and Prismic run it for you. Markstage is self-hosted only.
  • Years of use. Most of these products have been in production for years, with bigger communities and plugin ecosystems. Markstage is pre-release.
  • Frameworks other than Astro. Markstage's core has no framework in it, but Astro is the only adapter so far.
