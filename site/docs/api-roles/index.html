Custom roles

Beyond the five built-in roles: a custom role for a translator, a reviewer or an editor who may not upload, limited to collections, locales and fields.

Part of the API contract (docs/API.md: its conventions, prefix and errors apply here). The five built-in roles (docs/API-PERMISSIONS.md) fit most sites. A custom role fits the rest: a translator who changes one locale's words of any page and nothing else, an author who also answers the site's forms, an editor who may not upload, a contractor who edits pages but never deletes during a freeze. Server code: packages/server/src/custom-roles/.

  • Built on a base role. A custom role names one of administrator, editor, author or contributor. A person given it has that base as their role (so every rank in the table still holds) and the custom role on top.
  • Rules in the promotion rules' shape. The same engine as Environments (docs/ENVIRONMENTS.md "Promotion rules", env/rules.mjs) decides which rules apply: action, what (kinds and paths), when (hours and freezes) and effect read the same in both, and are checked by the same code.
  • Deny wins; an allow adds; no rule leaves the base. A rule that denies takes a right away. One that allows gives a right the base lacks, but never one that is the owner's and administrators' only (managing people, the audit log, webhooks, code on the site, backups, rollbacks): those are refused when the role is saved. When no rule applies, the base role decides, as if there were no custom role.
  • In this environment. Roles live in the server's database, like its people, and are not promoted.
  • The owner and administrators make, change, remove and give them; a role built on administrator is the owner's alone to make, change, remove or give. Every change is audited (role.create, role.update, role.delete, user.update with customRole) and tells the administrators ("A custom role was made, changed or removed", kind roles.changed, a safety alert).

A role

{
  "id": "translator-fr",
  "name": "French translator",
  "base": "contributor",
  "rules": [
    { "id": "fr-words", "name": "Edits the French words of posts", "action": ["entry.edit"],
      "collections": ["posts"], "fields": ["locales.fr-ca.**"], "effect": "allow" },
    { "id": "no-live", "name": "Never publishes or deletes", "action": ["entry.publish", "entry.delete"], "effect": "deny" }
  ]
}
KeyWhat
id^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,39}$, not a built-in role's name; fixed once made
nameWhat people see (at most 60 characters)

A rule:

KeyWhat
id, nameUnique in the role; the name is how a refusal explains itself
actionEntry actions entry.create, entry.edit, entry.publish (also schedule and unpublish), entry.delete, entry.rawHtml; or abilities by name (inbox.read, media.upload, site.publish, …: GET roles lists them with the least built-in role that has each)
effectallow or deny
collectionsEntry actions only: the collections it is about (default: all)
ownEntry actions only: true = only the person's own entries (their authorSlug is the entry's author)
whatEntry actions only: {only, not, any} of kinds (pages, menus, media, data, content, …) or paths (src/content/blog/**), as in the promotion rules
when{windows: [{days, from, to, tz}], freezes: [{from, until?, words?}]}, as in the promotion rules: an allow applies only inside its hours and not during a freeze; a deny with freezes applies only during one
fieldsOne allow rule on entry.edit only: the keys its edit may change, as dotted globs (locales.fr-ca.**, seo.*, title); body is the Markdown body. When every rule that allows an edit names fields, a save that changes anything else is 403 with {allowed, outside}

Things to know:

  • A live entry. Saving a live or scheduled entry needs the publish right (the change goes live). A rule of the person's own role that allows entry.edit on it covers that too, without the publish right: a translator corrects live pages' words but cannot publish, schedule or unpublish them.
  • Someone else's entry. An allow without own lets the person save others' entries in its scope; the entry keeps its author (a non-editor otherwise only writes under their own name).
  • The editor knows. An entry's can (GET collections/:c/entries/:id) then has fields: the globs its edit may change, so the editor shows the rest read-only (packages/editor/src/field-access.ts, the same matching as the server): a banner says what the role changes; the title, the text and its blocks' settings, and every Post settings field outside the globs are greyed out but still readable and copyable (a picture setting counts when every key it writes is covered); "Draft translation" offers only the regions it covers; the Source view is read-only (it is the whole file); a save sends only the covered keys. A page in the pages editor does the same (packages/editor/src/pages): every change goes through the page model, which keeps only the covered keys; the panel's fields outside the globs are greyed out, the page's words outside them can't be typed into on the canvas, and unless the role covers sections no section can be added, moved, duplicated or deleted (the outline, the grip menu and drops on the canvas say why). A person who may only read the page (can.edit false) sees all of it greyed out, with that reason.
  • The server's own stamps don't count. A page's modified date is set by the server on every save, so it is not a field the person changed: a role that covers sections saves a section edit without also needing modified.
  • Fields come from the save itself. The check compares the file before and after, whichever route the text came by (the editor, the source view, an import, an agent), so it cannot be gone around.
  • Agents. An AI assistant's token (docs/API-MCP.md) acts as its person, custom role included: a translator's agent changes the same words and nothing else. What an agent may read follows the base role (docs/API-MCP.md "Reads follow the role").

Routes

RouteBodyAnswer
GET roles{builtin, bases, custom: [role], actions: {entry, abilities: [{name, min}]}, kinds, collections: [{id, label}]}
POST rolesa role201 {role}
PUT roles/:rid{name, base, rules, version}{role}; 409 with current when it changed since it was loaded. A new base moves its people to that rank
DELETE roles/:rid{ok, people}: the people with it keep its base role
POST roles/check{userId, action, collection?, id?}{user, action, allowed, by: 'rule' | 'base', rule, fields, words, trace: [{rule, matched, why}]}: a dry run of what that person may do now, and why

A role as answered: {id, name, base, rules, version, people, updatedAt}. A mistake is 422 invalid with field and the reason in words, for example "users.manage stays with the owner and administrators: a custom role may take it away (deny), not give it."

In the editor, Settings → Roles (#/settings/roles) lists the roles with their rules as sentences, makes and changes them (?role=<id>, ?role=new, a rule's form at &rule=<id>, the changes kept for the tab until Save) and asks "What may they do?" (&as=<uid>&do=<action>&in=<collection>&entry=<id>: POST roles/check, with each rule's part). A role built on administrator shows only to the owner as one to change. Proof: e2e/roles.e2e.mjs.

A person is given a role with PATCH users/:uid {customRole: "<id>"} (docs/API.md "Users"); {role: "<built-in>"} takes it away. User.customRole is {id, name} or null.

Tests: packages/server/test/custom-roles.test.mjs.
