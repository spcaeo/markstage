This guide is an MDX file. Markstage opens it like any post: you type the words on the page, and the parts that are code stay exactly as they are written.

<Callout type="tip" title="Try it">
  Click this box. Its settings open on the right: change the type to **warn** and the box turns orange when you save.
</Callout>

## Components are cards

A component shows as a card with its name and settings. Text settings, numbers and on/off switches change in the side panel; a setting that is code, like the chart's data below, is shown as written.

<Chart data={saves} height={160} unit="saves" />

## What stays as written

The imports at the top, the `saves` list and anything in curly braces are code. Change them in Source mode, or ask a developer. A save never touches them: it writes back only the words and settings you changed.
